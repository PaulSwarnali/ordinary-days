Hi, My Love.

This isn't for our anniversary.

It's not your birthday.

And it's definitely not because I needed a reason.

I just woke up today... and wanted to make something that would make you smile.

Ordinary Days

People think love lives inside grand gestures.

I don't think so.

I think love is remembering how you like your coffee.

Saving a meme because "I know she'll laugh."

Or seeing something beautiful and immediately wishing you were there.

Something I Hope You Know

I'm still learning.

Still growing.

Still becoming the partner I want to be.

I won't always get everything right.

But I hope one thing never changes—

I'll never stop trying.

My Favourite Future

When I think about the future...

I don't imagine perfection.

I imagine grocery shopping together.

Arguing over what to eat.

Watching movies we'll probably never finish.

Growing older... without ever growing apart.

Thank You.

Thank you...

For loving me.

For laughing with me.

For believing in us.

Being with you makes me want to become a kinder, stronger, better partner every single day.

Just One More Thing...

I hope, years from now, we somehow find this page again.

And smile.

Because everything written here will still be true.

You are my favourite person.

My safest place.

And the best part of my ordinary days.

With all my love,
Swarnali 💚
